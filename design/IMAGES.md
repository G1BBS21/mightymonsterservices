## Style rules for new imagery

Match the existing set:

- Gritty, documentary look
- Golden-hour or overcast Central Texas light
- Slate shadows
- Sun-yellow safety vests
- No readable text or logos
- Generic trailer (real branding to be added later)

## Alt text

Describe what's shown, e.g. "Crew loading an old couch into a trailer". While images are
AI-generated, don't name a real town or customer in the alt text: that would describe a real
job that didn't happen.
